### What this settles about image weight

`/turtle-haven` transfers **17.4 MB**. Its HTML is ~1.3 MB uncompressed / 243 KB
gzipped. So **the images are ~98% of the page** and the ~1 MB HTML shell —
finding 4, and the headline of this whole audit — is a rounding error against
them on the live site.

It also resolves the upper-bound caveat on Grok's image-payload measurement.
Grok summed the referenced `static.wixstatic.com` asset URLs at **70.7 MB** for
this page and flagged it as an upper bound, because a browser requests Wix
resize/quality variants rather than the raw URLs. Measured reality is **17.4 MB**
— about **4× lower**. The caveat was correct and the bound was loose. Quote
17.4 MB; do not quote 70.7 MB as page weight.

`/book-turtle-haven` at **12.9 MB** is the worst offender relative to its job: a
booking page carrying 12.9 MB to deliver ~125 words and a client-injected widget.

## The finding this data produces

**Almost none of this traffic is qualified, and the house names are why.**

Read the top 20 as intent rather than volume:

- **`sea haven st augustine rental` — 158 impressions.** St. Augustine is ~150
  miles north of Melbourne Beach. Someone searching it cannot be served by this
  business at all.
- `sea haven rentals` (251), `sea haven` (206), `seahaven rentals` (43),
  `sea haven florida` (25), `beach haven villas` (22), `beach haven rentals with
  pool` (21), `ocean haven`, `seaside haven`, `tropical haven melbourne florida`
  — *Sea Haven*, *Beach Haven* and *Shell Haven* are among the most common
  vacation-rental names in the United States. These impressions are collisions
  with other businesses' brands, not demand for this one.
- **Average position 33.7** — page four. 1,458 named impressions produced
  **4 clicks** in a year.
- **Actual brand search is tiny but converts:** `the florida havens` → 13
  impressions, **1 click** in 29 days. That is a ~7.7% CTR against 0.3%
  sitewide.
- **Place-and-intent queries are almost absent from the top 20.** No
  `melbourne beach vacation rental`, no `indialantic beachfront rental`, no
  `beachfront rental near kennedy space center` — despite the site running ten
  demand pages aimed at exactly those. `tropical haven melbourne florida` (14) is
  the only place-qualified entry, and it is someone else's brand.

### What follows from it

1. **The property pages must never compete on house name alone.** "Sea Haven" is
   not a differentiator; "Sea Haven, Indialantic FL, beachfront" is. Titles in
   this rebuild have been changed accordingly — see below.
2. **The ten demand pages are the right strategy and are not yet working.**
   They are the only assets targeting qualified intent, and they are invisible.
   That is consistent with every page on the site declaring its `<h1>` to be
   "HOME ABOUT PROPERTIES…" (P0.1) — this baseline is what P0.1 is meant to move.
3. **This is the first honest conversion datum in the project:** brand search
   converts ~25× better than the generic tail. Capturing brand demand is not the
   growth lever, though — 13 impressions is the whole market for it.
4. **Do not read the +11% / +19% on the 90-day card as progress.** Nothing had
   been changed yet at capture time.

### Title change made in response

`content/properties.ts` previously used `Sea Haven | The Florida Havens` — no
place, competing directly with the national "Sea Haven" tail above. Now
place-qualified, still ≤60 characters (CI-gated):

| Property | Title |
|---|---|
| Turtle Haven | `Turtle Haven | Beachfront Rental, Melbourne Beach FL` |
| Shell Haven | `Shell Haven | Beachfront Rental, Melbourne Beach FL` |
| Beach Haven | `Beach Haven | Beachfront Rental, Indialantic FL` |
| Sea Haven | `Sea Haven | Beachfront Rental, Indialantic FL` |
| The Havens at The Dunes | `The Havens at The Dunes | Beachfront, Melbourne Beach FL` |
| The Havens at Beach Street | `The Havens at Beach Street | Beachfront, Indialantic FL` |

Dropping the `| The Florida Havens` suffix is deliberate: brand search is 13
impressions and is already served by the homepage and the site-wide
`LodgingBusiness` JSON-LD. The characters buy a place qualifier, which the data
says is the actual gap.
